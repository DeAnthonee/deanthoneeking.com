Under the hood

The System

The game room isn't an embed or a demo reel — it's a small production fleet, and this site is its front door.

Self-hosted fleet

Every game runs as its own service on always-on hardware I operate, each on its own subdomain.

Zero exposure

Nothing listens on the open internet — traffic arrives through a secure Cloudflare tunnel.

Continuous deploys

A push to a game's repo is rebuilt, test-gated, and live within minutes. Failed tests never ship.

Graceful updates

Servers drain cleanly — some snapshot live matches, so a mid-game deploy means players keep their seats.
